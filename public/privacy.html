Privacy Policy - Cong Lake Shore Text Messages

Cong Lake Shore, 350 Lake Shore Drive, Monroe, NY 10950, runs a text message program for its members and community to send shul announcements and daily davening times.

What we collect

Your name and mobile phone number when you sign up at shul-texts.vercel.app/join or text JOIN to (844) 456-5253, and any messages you send to that number.

How we use it

Only to send you Cong Lake Shore text messages and to answer messages you send us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or opt-in information with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information is shared only with our text message provider as needed to deliver messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to (844) 456-5253 at any time to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.
