Messages from members

Members text REPLY: their message to the shul number. Answer here, or the gabbai can answer by text from his phone.

Davening times (TIMES)

Maariv = Rabbeinu Tam (72 min after shkia). Times change every day on their own.
The same time is sent every day until you change it.

Special message for a day (or a few days)

For days that are different (early Mincha, Chol Hamoed...). From the first day to the last day, TIMES sends this message instead. For one day, leave "To" empty. It's removed by itself after the last day passes.

Friday & Erev Yom Tov zmanim

On Friday and Erev Yom Tov, a member who texts TIMES gets the zmanim instead: הדלקת הנירות, סוף זמן ק״ש, and מוצאי שבת / יום טוב. When Yom Tov is two days (or next to Shabbos), all the days come in one text. Nothing is sent automatically. A special message (above) for that day still wins.

מוצאי שבת / יום טוב = 72 minutes after shkia (Rabbeinu Tam).

Minyan count

Text "Who is coming to Shacharis / Mincha / Maariv?". Members answer ME, YES, X2, X3 (or NO). When the count reaches the goal, everyone who was asked gets a text that there is a minyan. One count runs at a time; it closes when you stop it, start a new one, or after 18 hours.

In the texts below, {tefila} and {when} are filled in (like "Shacharis tomorrow").

Auto-schedule

When "Auto-schedule is on" above, once a day (around 7 AM) the system checks these date ranges and automatically sends the count for any that include today - as long as nothing is already running and it hasn't already sent one today.

Gabbai

REPLY messages are texted to this number. The gabbai answers by texting back (goes to the last member), or #1234 message to answer a specific member. To send a member's message to everyone, he replies Y, then YES ALL to confirm.
It's read out twice, then the call ends. (Twilio must send calls to https://shul-texts.vercel.app/api/inbound.)

Auto-replies

Keep JOIN on its own line (or in its own sentence) - it's left out for people who already joined. Same for NAME - left out for people whose name we have.
Members can write REPLY: or just R (like "R can I get an aliyah?"). Texting only REPLY or R means their next text goes to the gabbai. STOP, START and HELP replies are sent by Twilio automatically.

What's new

Each update is its own box. The gabbai sees them when he presses What's new at the top - newest on top, with a red New tag until he opens it.
