Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Congregation Lake Shore ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you sign up for and use our SMS text messaging program.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run our text messaging program: your name and your mobile phone number, which you provide when you opt in to receive messages.

How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number solely to send you the messages you signed up for, including congregation announcements, minyan and davening times, event reminders, and community updates. We also use your information to respond to your inquiries and to comply with our legal obligations.

SMS Text Messaging Program

Congregation Lake Shore operates an SMS text messaging program to send announcements, minyan and davening times, event reminders, and community updates to members who have opted in. By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you consent to receive these text messages. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can reply HELP for assistance.

Your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. We use your phone number only to deliver the messages you have signed up to receive.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We use a messaging service provider only to deliver the text messages you have requested.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Your information is stored within the secure systems of our messaging service provider.

Your Choices

You may opt out of the text messaging program at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may contact us with any questions about your information using the details below.

Contact Us
